Professional Summary

Ph.D. candidate working on generative modeling, reasoning, and representation learning, including LLM and VLM reasoning, inverse problems, latent alignment, image and video generation, and visual world models, with a further interest in medical imaging.

Projects

  • 2025 - 2026
    Low-Rank Hierarchical Merging for Efficient Long-to-Short Reasoning

    Training-free, layer-adaptive merging of long chain-of-thought models with base LLMs, guided by Hessian-based layer sensitivity. NeurIPS 2026.

    • 48.8% average accuracy on six math benchmarks (Qwen 1.5B), 3.2 points above ORCA-AIM, with 59.7% shorter responses.
    • Generalizes to Llama and Mistral model pairs; keeps 96.8% of R1’s HumanEval accuracy with 62% shorter responses.
  • 2026
    CLIMB-Flow: Coupled Bayesian Posterior Sampling via Multiscale Flow Models

    Training-free posterior sampler for linear inverse problems with cascaded pixel-space flow models, using split Gibbs-Langevin dynamics with conjugate-gradient preconditioning.

    • Proved a generalized Tweedie identity for linear-Gaussian flow interpolants.
    • Best LPIPS on 3 of 5 ImageNet tasks; 28.10 dB PSNR and 0.907 SSIM on 8x accelerated fastMRI.
  • 2025 - 2026
    FlowAlign: Flow-Guided Target-Space Alignment via Path Consistency

    Two-stage multimodal alignment that reuses a frozen conditional flow field as a path-consistency supervisor. NeurIPS 2026 (co-first author).

    • Best LTA, NMI, and ARI on the PBMC and BMMC single-cell benchmarks; first on all reported metrics for DINOv3-to-SigLIP2 image-text alignment (CUBICC).
    • As a plug-in PeakVI-to-scVI regularizer, reduced FOSCTTM from 0.548 to 0.068.
  • 2025
    Variational Diffusion Recovery with Multi-scale Energy Models (VDRME)

    Optimal-transport (JKO) view of multiscale diffusion recovery; each proximal step is amortized by a conditional generator for few-step energy-based generation.

    • FID 6.90 on CIFAR-10 with 8 steps and 4.62 on class-conditional ImageNet-256 with 4 steps, without classifier guidance or distillation.
  • 2025
    Uni-Pose

    Three-stage pose estimation framework that aligns FLUX.1 Kontext latents with a GroupPose-based sparse keypoint decoder for human and animal poses.

    • Outperformed the re-implemented GroupPose baseline with strong cross-dataset and cross-species generalization (COCO, MPII, AP-10K).
